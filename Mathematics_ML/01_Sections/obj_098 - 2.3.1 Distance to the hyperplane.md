# 2.3.1 Distance to the hyperplane

## Conceptual overview
This subsection derives the distance from a point $x$ to a hyperplane $w^\top u + b = 0$. It uses two approaches: a geometric argument based on projecting the point onto the normal vector $w$, and a dual approach using the recently developed duality theory.

## Why it matters
The concept of margin (distance to decision boundary) is central to SVMs, making this geometric result crucial.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What is the formula for the distance from $x$ to the hyperplane $w^\top u + b = 0$?
> The distance is $\frac{|w^\top x + b|}{\|w\|}$.

## Narrative flow
The subsection presents the problem, performs a direct geometric projection derivation, and then re-derives the same result using Lagrange duality as an illustrative exercise.
