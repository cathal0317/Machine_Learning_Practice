# Contents

## Conceptual overview
This section provides a structured roadmap of the lecture notes, divided into three primary chapters: Statistical Learning, Optimization, and Neural Networks. It outlines the progression from fundamental risk definitions and Bayes classifiers to advanced generative models like GANs and VAEs. The table of contents serves as a guide for navigating the mathematical foundations and algorithmic implementations covered in the module.

## Why it matters
Understanding the module structure helps students see the logical progression from theoretical statistical foundations to practical optimization algorithms and modern deep learning architectures.
