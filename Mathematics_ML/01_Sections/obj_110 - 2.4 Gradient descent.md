# 2.4 Gradient descent

## Conceptual overview
Gradient descent is presented as an iterative method for finding function minima where analytical solutions are infeasible. The section covers the core algorithm, the direction of steepest descent, selection of learning rates, and rigorous convergence rates under various assumptions like strong convexity and smoothness.

## Why it matters
Understanding the mechanics and theoretical limits of gradient descent is essential for any deep study of machine learning optimization.

## Active recall
> [!question]- Why is gradient descent necessary?
> Because for many complex objectives (like those in deep learning), we cannot solve the optimality condition $\nabla f = 0$ in closed form.

## Narrative flow
The subsection starts with motivating iterative methods, defines the algorithm, discusses stepsize heuristics, and then provides a deep dive into formal convergence analysis for different classes of functions.
