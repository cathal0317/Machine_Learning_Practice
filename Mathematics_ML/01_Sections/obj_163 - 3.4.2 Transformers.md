# 3.4.2 Transformers

## Conceptual overview
Transformers are neural network architectures designed primarily for natural language processing that operate on sequences of vectors called tokens. They rely on self-attention layers to capture relationships between elements regardless of their distance in the sequence.

## Why it matters
They have become the dominant architecture for NLP and generative large language models, offering superior scaling and modeling of long-range dependencies compared to RNNs or CNNs.

## Active recall
> [!question]- How are tokens typically enhanced with spatial information in a Transformer?
> Each token $x_i$ might contain positional information, such as a number indicating its relative position, appended as the last entry in its vector.

## Narrative flow
The section moves from defining tokens and contexts to the mathematical formulation of self-attention, extending it to multi-head self-attention (MHSA), and concluding with the compositional blocks of a Transformer layer.
