# 2.3.3 The dual problem for SVM

## Conceptual overview
This subsection applies Lagrange duality to the primal SVM problem. The resulting dual objective depends only on the inner products between training data points, and the solution reveals that the optimal weight vector is a linear combination of a subset of data points called 'support vectors'.

## Why it matters
The dual formulation is what enables the 'kernel trick' and provides an efficient way to solve SVMs when the number of features is large.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What are 'support vectors' in the context of the dual SVM problem?
> Support vectors are the data points for which the corresponding dual multiplier $\xi_i$ is non-zero; these points lie exactly on the margin boundary.

## Narrative flow
The subsection constructs the Lagrangian, applies first-order optimality conditions to eliminate the primal variables, and formulates the resulting dual maximization problem.
