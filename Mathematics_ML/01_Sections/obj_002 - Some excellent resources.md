# Some excellent resources

## Conceptual overview
This section identifies key external academic texts and online resources that provide additional depth or alternative perspectives on machine learning mathematics. It references works by authors such as Martin Lotz, Rajen Shah, Mehryar Mohri, Francis Bach, and Gabriel Peyre. These resources are foundational to the module's approach, combining rigorous probability theory with modern computational data science.

## Why it matters
External resources allow students to cross-reference notation, find additional proofs, and explore practical coding implementations (like those mentioned for JAX or PyTorch) not fully detailed in the primary notes.
