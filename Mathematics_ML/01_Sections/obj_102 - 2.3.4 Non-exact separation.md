# 2.3.4 Non-exact separation

## Conceptual overview
To handle noise and overlapping classes, the hard constraints of SVM are relaxed using slack variables $s_i$ that measure individual classification errors. The objective is modified to balance margin maximization with error minimization, which is equivalent to minimizing the 'Hinge loss'.

## Why it matters
Real-world data is rarely perfectly separable; this extension makes SVMs robust and practically useful.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What is the role of the parameter $\mu$ in soft-margin SVM?
> The parameter $\mu$ (sometimes called $C$) controls the trade-off between maximizing the margin and minimizing the total training error (misfit).

## Narrative flow
The narrative identifies the limitation of the hard-margin SVM, introduces slack variables to allow mistakes, and shows the equivalence of this formulation to Hinge loss minimization.
