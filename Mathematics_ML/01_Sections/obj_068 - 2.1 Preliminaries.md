# 2.1 Preliminaries

## Conceptual overview
This section sets the stage for optimization in machine learning by defining types of minimizers (local, global, strict) and introducing convexity as a key property that makes optimization tractable. It establishes that for convex functions, local search is sufficient because local optima are guaranteed to be global.

## Why it matters
Most machine learning training procedures are formulated as optimization problems; understanding these foundations is critical for algorithm design.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What is the goal of the optimization problems discussed here?
> The goal is to devise efficient iterative algorithms to approximate a minimizer $w^* \in \text{argmin } f(w)$ with low computational cost per iteration.

## Narrative flow
The subsection starts with motivating examples like linear regression and classification losses. It then defines the different flavors of minimizers before launching into a thorough treatment of convexity, including first and second-order characterizations.
