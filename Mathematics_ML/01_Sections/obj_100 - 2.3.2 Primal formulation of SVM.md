# 2.3.2 Primal formulation of SVM

## Conceptual overview
Starting from the geometric margin, this subsection shows that maximizing the margin is equivalent to minimizing $\|w\|$ subject to the constraint that all data points are correctly classified with a functional margin of at least 1. This results in a convex quadratic program with linear inequality constraints.

## Why it matters
It translates the intuitive goal of 'large margin' into a concrete mathematical problem that can be solved using standard optimization tools.

## Active recall
> [!question]- How is the primal SVM optimization problem defined?
> It is defined as $\text{argmin}_{w, b} \|w\|$ s.t. $Y_i(w^\top X_i + b) \ge 1$ for all $i = 1, \dots, n$.

## Narrative flow
The derivation begins with the raw margin expression, uses scale invariance to simplify the constraints, and arrives at the standard norm-minimization form.
