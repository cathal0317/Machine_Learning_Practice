# 3.6.1 Dimension reduction

## Conceptual overview
Dimension reduction is the process of mapping high-dimensional data into a lower-dimensional 'latent' space while preserving essential information. It is motivated by needs for visualization, noise reduction, and computational efficiency.

## Why it matters
High-dimensional data is often difficult to process and redundant; finding a compact representation simplifies downstream tasks and reveals hidden structure.

## Active recall
> [!question]- List three reasons why dimension reduction is desirable.
> To visualize data more easily, to remove noise, and to lower resource requirements for storage/processing.

## Narrative flow
Starts with the motivation for dimensionality reduction, introduces autoencoders as a specific approach, and presents PCA as the optimal linear case.
