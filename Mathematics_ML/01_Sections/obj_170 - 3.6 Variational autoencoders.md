# 3.6 Variational autoencoders

## Conceptual overview
Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) extend the deterministic autoencoder into a probabilistic framework. They treat the encoding and decoding processes as sampling from conditional distributions, enabling generative capabilities by sampling from the latent space.

## Why it matters
VAEs provide a mathematically rigorous way to perform dimensionality reduction while simultaneously learning a generative model of the data.

## Active recall
> [!question]- How does a VAE differ from a standard autoencoder?
> A standard autoencoder uses deterministic mappings for encoding and decoding, whereas a VAE models these as conditional distributions $q_\theta(z|x)$ and $p_\theta(x|z)$.
