# 1.6 The estimation error

## Conceptual overview
This large section details how to bound the estimation error, which is the random variable $R(\hat{h}) - \inf_{h \in \mathcal{H}} R(h)$. It establishes the machinery for moving from finite to infinite hypothesis classes using concentration inequalities, Rademacher complexity, and VC dimension.

## Why it matters
Bounding estimation error is the 'heart' of statistical learning theory, as it tells us how many samples are needed for a model to generalize.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What is the goal of the 'Estimation Error' section?
> To quantify how the gap between empirical risk and true risk depends on the number of samples $n$ and the size/complexity of the hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}$.

## Narrative flow
The section progresses from basic union bounds for finite classes to rigorous non-asymptotic concentration tools, then introduces Rademacher complexity for infinite classes, and finally VC dimension as a combinatorial way to control that complexity.
