# 3.6.2 Variational autoencoders

## Conceptual overview
Variational Autoencoders model data and latent variables using conditional distributions $q_\theta(z|x)$ and $p_\theta(x|z)$. They optimize a surrogate objective called the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) to handle the intractability of direct likelihood maximization.

## Why it matters
This subsection develops the full mathematical engine for one of the most popular generative models in machine learning.

## Active recall
> [!question]- How is a sample in the data space generated in a VAE?
> First, draw a sample $z \sim p_Z$ from the latent prior, then sample from the conditional distribution $p_\theta(\cdot|z)$.

## Narrative flow
Moves from the probabilistic definition to MLE intractability, the derivation of the ELBO, the interpretation of the resulting loss terms, and finally the gradient computation techniques.
