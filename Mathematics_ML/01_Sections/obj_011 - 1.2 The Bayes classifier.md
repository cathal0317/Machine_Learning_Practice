# 1.2 The Bayes classifier

## Conceptual overview
This subsection introduces the 'gold standard' for classification: the Bayes classifier $h^*$. It is defined as the hypothesis that achieves the absolute minimum possible misclassification risk. By analyzing the conditional distribution of labels $Y$ given features $X$, the notes show that the optimal strategy is always to predict the most probable class.

## Why it matters
The Bayes classifier represents the upper bound on performance (the 'Oracle' performance) for any machine learning algorithm.

## Active recall
> [!question]- How is the Bayes classifier formally defined?
> The Bayes classifier is $h^* := \text{argmin}_h R(h)$, where $R(h)$ is the misclassification risk.

## Narrative flow
Moves from the goal (minimizing misclassification) to the technical review of conditional probability, leading into the characterization of the optimal decision boundary (Proposition 1).
