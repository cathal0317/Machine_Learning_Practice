# Chapter 3 Neural networks

## Conceptual overview
This chapter provides a comprehensive introduction to deep learning. It covers the architectural design of neural networks, the theoretical limits of their expressiveness (Universal Approximation), the computational methods for training them (backpropagation), and specific advanced frameworks like CNNs and generative models.

## Why it matters
Neural networks are the state-of-the-art approach for most modern machine learning applications; this chapter explains both why they work and how they are implemented.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What are the main components of the neural networks chapter?
> The chapter covers MLPs and expressiveness, loss functions (cross-entropy), automatic differentiation (backpropagation), common architectures (CNNs, Transformers), and generative models (GANs, VAEs).

## Narrative flow
The section progresses from the basic building blocks (MLPs) to the theoretical power of networks (universal approximation), then to the technical details of training (differentiation), and finally to specialized architectures and generative modeling.
