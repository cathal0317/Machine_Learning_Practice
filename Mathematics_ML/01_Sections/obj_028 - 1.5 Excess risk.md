# 1.5 Excess risk

## Conceptual overview
This section introduces the study of excess risk as the fundamental metric for learning performance. It addresses how the complexity of the hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}$ and the number of data points $n$ influence this risk.

## Why it matters
The section sets the stage for formal statistical learning theory by defining the gap between practical estimators and theoretical optima.

## Active recall
> [!question]- What are the two key questions addressed in the Excess Risk section?
> 1. How does the complexity of $\mathcal{H}$ influence excess risk? 2. How does the number of data points $n$ influence excess risk?

## Narrative flow
The section defines excess risk, states the No Free Lunch theorem to establish lower bounds, provides Example 4 to demonstrate upper bounds via sample complexity, and concludes with the decomposition of excess risk.
